## 改写示意

原稿：`Premium Bath Brush, Best Quality, Perfect for Everyone`

问题：泛泛夸赞占用空间；“适合所有人”没有依据；买家看不到结构与数量。

改写：`Long Handle Bath Brush with Hanging Loop, 1 Count`

Highlights：`Long handle with hanging loop. Includes one bath brush. Rinse after use and hang to dry.`

完整演示稿见 [listing.json](listing.json)。其中最后一条五点用于展示“资料缺口”，不是可发布文案；补齐实际资料后再写对应卖点。

## FAQ 演示

**How many brushes are included?**

This fictional example includes one bath brush. No additional items are included.

**How is the brush stored after use?**

The demonstration care instructions say to rinse the brush and hang it to dry using the loop. Follow the actual product instructions when preparing a real listing.

**Are the bristles soft or suitable for sensitive skin?**

The teaching brief does not provide bristle firmness or skin-suitability evidence. These details must be confirmed before making a claim.
